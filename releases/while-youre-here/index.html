Some people leave before they ever walk out the door.

While You're Here is the debut single from Mila Delren — a soulful, late-night R&B song about desire, vulnerability, and the quiet moments when love asks us to stop running.

A coat rests on a chair. Keys are still in his hand. He's standing by the door, caught somewhere between leaving and staying.

She doesn't need another beautiful promise about forever. She needs something more honest: his presence, right here, right now.

“Put your coat on the chair. Take your hand off the door.”

It's a simple request, but sometimes the hardest thing to give someone isn't love. It's the courage to stay.

A Late-Night R&B Slow Jam

Built around warm, velvety female vocals, a smooth, understated groove, and an intimate atmosphere, While You're Here captures the emotional tension between attraction and uncertainty.

The song unfolds with a sense of closeness, leaving room for every hesitation, every breath, and every unspoken feeling.

It's contemporary R&B made for quiet drives after midnight, evenings together, and those moments when the conversation stops but the emotions don't.

A Love Story Drawn in Pencil

The official animated music video brings the story to life through cinematic black-and-white pencil sketches.

As each scene gradually takes shape, the drawings reveal the small details that make the song feel personal: a doorway, a lingering glance, the distance between two people, and the possibility of closing it.

The minimalist visual style mirrors the music itself — intimate, emotional, and focused on what matters.

The Moment Between Leaving and Staying

While You're Here is about wanting someone without losing yourself in the uncertainty of their feelings.

It's about drawing a line between affection and half-hearted commitment. About asking for honesty instead of reassurance. And about recognizing that sometimes the most meaningful choice is simply to stay.

If you've ever wished someone would put down their keys, forget about the door, and be fully present with you, this song is for you.
